Compare. Evaluate. Improve.

A Ministry of Health, Zambia programme supporting medical laboratories across the Copperbelt to strengthen
quality, reliability, and continual improvement through Interlaboratory Comparison (ILC).

The programme provides a centralized platform for laboratories to receive samples, submit examination results,
and receive professional performance feedback, supporting quality requirements of ISO 15189:2022.

Government/Public Laboratories

Participating public-sector laboratories connected through a
structured comparison programme.

Private Medical Laboratories

Participating private laboratories contributing to shared quality
improvement and performance comparison.

Strengthening laboratory quality through collaboration, comparison, and continual improvement.
